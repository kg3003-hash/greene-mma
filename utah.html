The Promotions

Who runs the cards here

SteelFist Fight Night

Union Event Center · Salt Lake City

Utah's longest-running live combat sports show. Mixed cards — MMA, kickboxing, and Muay Thai on the same night — and the most reliable place in the state to watch a prospect's third pro fight.

Fierce Fighting Championship

Mountain America Center · Regional

Built on the bones of Showdown Fights, and currently the biggest promotion operating in the region. Heavyweight-heavy cards and a real title picture.

UFC at the Delta Center

Salt Lake City · Occasional

Four visits and counting, including UFC 278, 291, and 307 — the top three grossing events in building history. When the UFC comes to town, the whole local scene gets a week of daylight.
