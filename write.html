House style

  • Dry and cageside. Like the sharpest person in the group chat who actually watches the prelims.
  • Short sentences. Real stakes.
  • Specific beats generic. Never "a local gym" — name the gym.
  • One smirk per piece, maximum. Most pieces need none.
  • No hype words. Not "shocking", not "massive", not "insane".
  • If there's an underdog angle, it gets the last line.
  • Never quote a source at length. Say it in your own words and link out.
  • Never invent a result, a round, or a method. If it's unclear, leave it out.
